# Case 10 — Cutting stock with finite remnants

Decision: choose integer cutting patterns from new stock and a limited remnant inventory.

Pattern generation includes saw kerf between pieces. Stock types have different acquisition values; remnant quantities are finite. Demand coverage is linked to explicit overproduction variables, and trim loss is charged in the objective.

Baseline: first-fit-style cutting from new 1000-unit bars only.

Audit: finished-piece demand and remnant availability are checked independently.

KPIs: total material objective and trim waste.
